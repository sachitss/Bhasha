Bhasha 1.1.0 – German · English · Nepali · Korean

- New language: Korean (한국어). Learn it from German, English or Nepali, or use it as your known language
- All 86 words, travel phrases and phrases of the day in Hangul with romanisation and example sentences; Korean-specific notes (number systems, family words)
- Native Korean voices built in: SunHi (female) and InJoon (male)
- Korean interface (앱 언어: 한국어), independent of the language you learn
- Includes everything from 1.0.3
